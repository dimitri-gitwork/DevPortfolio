A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Development meets creativity.

My experience spans independent software development and professional development environments. I've worked on casino game development using C# at ISD Games and interactive 3D data visualization at United States Performance Center.

At United States Performance Center, I created 3D models in Blender and integrated them into interactive web experiences using React Three Fiber, JavaScript, and HTML. This included a 3D avatar that allowed clients to visualize workout results and performance metrics through an interactive model rather than relying solely on traditional data sheets.

Outside of my professional experience, I independently develop games and applications, exploring technologies such as Firebase, APIs, AI integrations, and interactive software development. One of my projects uses Google's Gemini API to bring AI-powered virtual staging to real estate photography.
